SWEEP INTENT FEE

The intent fee is feeBps basis points of the swept amount, signed into the EIP-712 SweepIntent by the stealth key itself. SweeperV2 pays it to tx.origin, not msg.sender: in a batched relay msg.sender is the BatchRelayer contract, while tx.origin is always the runner that actually paid the gas. That is what makes third-party relaying safe to open up: any runner can carry the intent, and the fee goes to whoever pays the gas.

  • MIN_FEE_BPS (default 30, 0.30%) is the relayer's floor: intents below it are rejected at preflight with a 400. Advertised at GET /fee and in GET /health so the app prices intents before signing.
  • MAX_FEE_BPS (1000, 10%) is the onchain cap enforced by SweeperV2 against the signed intent. No relayer can take more, ever.
  • The fee is computed on the full balance of the swept asset at execution time, in that asset.

PRIVACY POOLS WITHDRAWAL RELAY FEE

relayFeeBPS basis points of the withdrawn value, default maximum 25 (0.25%, PP_FEE_BPS). The groth16 circuit binds the fee terms into the proof itself: the context public signal commits to the withdrawal data, which contains the recipient, the fee recipient, and the fee rate. The relayer recomputes the context and rejects anything where the fee recipient is not one of its runners or the rate exceeds PP_FEE_BPS. The fee is not a separate transfer: the entrypoint pays it to the runner inside the withdrawal, so the exact amount is known from the withdrawnValue public signal.

RELAYER GAS COSTS

The runner pays gas on every relayed transaction. Gas pricing is a 2x bump over current network prices with a priority-fee floor (MIN_PRIORITY_WEI, default 0.1 gwei) so builders do not skip ultra-cheap transactions. The affordability preflight refuses broadcasts the runner cannot pay for. Fee economics in brief: the announce is free and costs the runner gas; the sweep fee and withdrawal fee are where the gas comes back.

THE FEE LEDGER (fees.jsonl)

Every successful fee-bearing broadcast appends one JSON line to fees.jsonl (gitignored, FEES_FILE overrides the path):

{"ts":"2026-09-12T…","kind":"sweep-intent","feeBps":30,"estFeeWei":"…","txHash":"0x…","runner":"0x…"}
KINDWRITTEN WHENestFeeWei
sweep-intentAn eip7702-intent sweep is broadcaststealth ETH balance × feeBps / 10000, estimated at preflight. Rough for token sweeps: the stealth ETH balance is not the swept asset.
sweep-intent-batchA batch sweep is broadcastOne line per swept stealth address, same estimate, all sharing one txHash.
pp-withdrawA withdrawal is relayedwithdrawnValue (public signal) × relayFeeBPS / 10000. Exact: this is what the entrypoint pays the runner.
fee-forwardThe auto-forwarder sweeps a runner's accrued fees to FEE_OWNERThe forwarded amount. An outflow, not revenue: excluded from earned totals, weekly buckets, and ARR.

Announces carry no fee and are never logged. A failed balance read skips the ledger line; it never blocks a broadcast. Legacy eip3009 and eip7702-sweep artifacts carry no relayer fee and write no lines.

THE REVENUE REPORT (fees.mjs)

node fees.mjs                        # report
FEES_FILE=/path/to.jsonl node fees.mjs

Report fields:

  • totals per kind: ETH and count for sweep-intent, sweep-intent-batch, pp-withdraw.
  • total: all earned fees in ETH, converted to USD at the current CoinGecko price (same source as GET /price, 60s cache, fails soft to ETH-only).
  • weekly: the last 8 Monday-starting weeks, ETH and count per week.
  • ARR run-rate: the sum of the last 4 weeks × 13 (52 / 4: four weeks annualized). Counts fees earned, not fees held.
  • fee-forward: total forwarded to the owner, forward count, timestamp of the last forward.
  • runner holdings: earned minus forwarded: what is still sitting on the runners.

Malformed ledger lines are skipped. Sweep fees are estimates; withdrawal fees are exact. For onchain truth, the runner balances and the FEE_OWNER address are the ledger of record.
