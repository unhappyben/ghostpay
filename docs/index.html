What it is

ghostpay is a privacy payments app on Ethereum mainnet. It receives to one-time ERC-5564 stealth addresses, sweeps the balance into Privacy Pools through a signed EIP-7702 intent, and withdraws to an unlinked wallet after ASP approval. The stealth address never holds gas and never sends a transaction itself: a relayer carries every step after payment, so the recipient's wallet appears nowhere onchain.

The journey

  1. 1GenerateOne wallet signature derives the stealth spend + view keys (ERC-5564 scheme 1). Same signature, same keys, any device. Nothing to back up.
  2. 2ShareThe app derives a fresh one-time address. Share the raw 0x address or a payment link.
  3. 3PayThe payer sends ETH and the announcement in one transaction via PayAndAnnounce. The relayer announces for raw-address payments.
  4. 4ScanThe viewing key scans Announcement logs and finds payments. The spend key is never needed to watch.
  5. 5SweepThe app signs an EIP-712 SweepIntent + a 7702 authorization; the relayer executes it against SweeperV2 and pays the gas. Funds land in Privacy Pools.
  6. 6WithdrawAfter ASP approval, a groth16 proof built in the browser withdraws to any fresh address, relayed gaslessly.
